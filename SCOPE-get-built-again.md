# SCOPE — "Get Built Again: Blueprint for 40+" (new ebook product)

**Status:** Scoping only (Beau approved scoping 2026-06-29). Not built.
**What it is:** The men's counterpart to the Femme Body 30-Day Slimdown — a
30-day, gym-equipment-based blueprint for guys 40+ getting back into training.

## Positioning
- Femme = home/bodyweight, fat-loss "slimdown" for women.
- This = **gym equipment** (not bodyweight), strength + rebuild for men 40+ who
  trained before and have fallen off. Angle: "get built *again*" — you've done it
  once, this is the smart way back without wrecking joints.
- Same anti-rebound, sustainable-habits DNA as the rest of Daniel's line.

## Proposed structure (mirror Femme so it's fast to produce)
1. Intro / who this is for (40+, returning lifter, time-poor).
2. The mindset + recovery reality of training over 40 (joints, sleep, protein).
3. Nutrition: TDEE, protein target, simple sustainable eating (reuse Femme macro logic, male calories).
4. **30 daily coaching videos** (Daniel to record, or reuse/adapt where topics overlap — many Femme topics are gender-neutral: TDEE, flexible dieting, plateaus, sleep, consistency).
5. **Gym workout program** — phased, equipment-based (barbell/dumbbell/machine), demo video per lift.
6. Recipe section (higher-calorie male versions).
7. Recov tie-in page (compliance-checked wording).

## What Daniel needs to supply

- Gym lift demo videos (the big differentiator — barbell/db/machine form clips).
- Confirm equipment assumption (full gym vs home gym vs minimal).
