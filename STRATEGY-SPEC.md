# STRATEGY-SPEC — Daniel Delahunty (PT coaching site)

Locked spec reconstructed from the foundation work that drove the build (the
reference-first process captured in the "what made the Daniel build work"
session) and written to disk 2026-06-12 to close the /perfect gate. The spec
below is what the shipped site implements.

## Who
Daniel Delahunty — Gold Coast personal trainer / online coach. Real client
results, real programmes (Rise Above 8-week, Lean For Life ongoing), in-person
GC training, plus a 7-ebook education line.

## Audience
Everyday adults (25–50) burned by crash diets and cookie-cutter programmes.
They've gotten results before and LOST them. Pain = the rebound, not the
starting.

## Positioning (the one idea)
**"Results you keep. Not just results you get."**
Anti-rebound coaching. Every page reinforces permanence over intensity:
sustainable habits, flexible dieting, education baked in. This is the wedge
against both hard-sell PT culture and app-only macro mills.

## Mechanism
- Rise Above = the 8-week on-ramp (structured win).
- Lean For Life = the keep-it system (ongoing coaching, "never lets you rebound").
- Ebook line (7 titles + bundle) = low-ticket education ladder feeding coaching.
- Macro calculator = free tool, capture/utility hook.

## Offer paths (every page must land on one)
1. Coaching enquiry (contact) — primary.
2. Programme pages (rise-above / lean-for-life / in-person) — mid intent.
3. Ebooks ($ low-ticket) + bundle — cold/self-serve.

## Voice
Plain, direct, second person. No bro-science, no shouting. Headlines state the
promise ("Coaching that actually sticks", "Simple, honest pricing").

## Build rules (from the locked feedback)
- Study the live reference + client's real material first; ground in foundation.
- Real photos only (clients/, daniel/ shoots) — no stock.
- Tiny increments; render + click + verify before "done".
- Education line pages share one template; bundle page sells the stack.

## Page map (as shipped)
index · coaching · rise-above · lean-for-life · in-person · pricing · results ·
ebooks (+7 ebook pages + bundle) · macros (working calculator) · faqs · contact.
