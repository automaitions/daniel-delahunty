Weekly billing — the mechanics

How the lock-in actually works.

A

The payment rail

Stripe weekly subscription with a 12-week schedule that rolls to week-to-week — or an AU direct-debit provider (Ezypay / Ezidebit), which is what gyms use and which chases failed payments for you.

B

The lock-in

Enforced by agreement, not by card tricks: clear minimum-term disclosure at signup, a cooling-off window, and a hardship clause. That combination is standard and ACL-fair in AU fitness.

C

Early exit

Leave before week 12 → remaining weeks settle at the casual rate (or a flat exit fee). After week 12 → cancel any time with a week's notice. Simple, defensible, no fine print.

Worth saying on the call and on the page: the minimum term is the product. People aren't paying for workouts — they're paying for the version of themselves that can't quietly quit in week three.
